FAQ

Is this tax advice?

No. The CSV is an on-chain record to hand to your accountant or tax software. Rules on worthless-asset losses differ by country.

Is the first reclaim really 0%?

Yes. Every wallet gets one empty token account (ATA) closed with a 0% platform fee as a lifetime proof. From ATA #2 onward the fee is 2.0%, charged atomically inside the same transaction you sign, so nothing is held by us.

How does the 30% ?ref= split work?

Add ?ref=<your Solana wallet> to any rentandrugshield.com link. When a referred user pays the 2.0% fee, 30% of that fee is routed on-chain to your wallet in the same transaction and 70% goes to the treasury. No dashboard, no manual payout.
