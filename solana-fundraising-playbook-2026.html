## 4. Seed & Mid-Stage Venture Capital on Solana ($1.5M to $25M+)

Once a Solana project graduates from MVP to measurable on-chain traction (daily active wallets, swap routing volume, protocol fees, or developer SDK installs), institutional venture capital unlocks scale capital for Tier-1 security audits, market-making liquidity, regulatory licensing, and engineering hires.

### Standard Deal Structures & Terms in 2026
1. **Early Seed ($1.5M to $4.5M):**
   * **Instrument:** Post-Money SAFE (YC standard) paired with a Token Warrant (or token side-letter) granting investors their pro-rata equity percentage of any future protocol token.
   * **Valuation Caps:** Typically **$15M to $35M** post-money depending on mainnet traction and founder pedigree.
   * **Lockups:** Investors and core team almost universally accept a **12-month cliff followed by 24 to 36 months of linear vesting** to protect secondary token markets.
2. **Mid-Stage / Series A ($6M to $25M+):**
   * **Instrument:** Priced Preferred Equity Round + Token Warrant (or direct SAFT for pure decentralized protocol foundations).
   * **Valuation Benchmarks:** **$50M to $150M+ FDV / post-money**, anchored to annualized protocol revenue (often 10x to 25x annualized net fees for DeFi/infra) or verified retention cohorts.
   * **Due Diligence Requirements:** Multiple independent Rust/Anchor or Token-2022 audits (OtterSec, Neodyme, Sec3, Trail of Bits), clean IP assignment, multisig treasury custody (Squads v4), and organic non-wash user retention.

## 5. Solana-Native On-Chain & Community Fundraising (Mid-Stage Alternative to VCs)

One of Solana's biggest breakthroughs in 2025 and 2026 is that mid-stage projects no longer have to rely exclusively on high-FDV, low-float private VC rounds. Three community-aligned capital models now thrive on Solana:

### A. Futarchy-Governed Treasury Raises via MetaDAO
Built natively on Solana, **MetaDAO** allows early and mid-stage projects (as well as investment DAOs like *mtnCapital*) to raise USDC directly into an on-chain treasury governed by **futarchy (conditional prediction markets)**:
* **How It Works:** Contributors deposit USDC in exchange for liquid project tokens, but founders cannot unilaterally drain the treasury. Major capital outlays or roadmap pivots pass only if prediction markets price the proposal as net-accretive to token value.
* **Why Investors Love It:** Eliminates blind "soft-rug" treasury risk and aligns mid-stage builders with liquid token holders from Day 1.

### B. Curated Angel & Merit Launchpads: Echo and Legion
* **Echo:** Allows Solana founders to raise $250k to $2M+ slices from curated angel syndicates (led by technical operators like Helius's Mert Mumtaz or DeFi founders) on identical terms to VCs, turning hundreds of power users into aligned evangelists.
* **Legion:** Uses verifiable on-chain history and GitHub/social developer reputation scores to allocate early and mid-stage rounds to real Solana builders and users rather than Sybil farm bots, with flexible vesting schedules.

### C. Fair-Price Discovery Pools: Meteora DLMM & Jupiter LFG
