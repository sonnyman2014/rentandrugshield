FAQ

Why is ClaimYourSol more expensive?

ClaimYourSol publishes fees from 5% up to 20% depending on wallet size. Rent & Rug Shield charges 0% on your first empty ATA and a flat 2.0% after that.

Is the first reclaim really 0%?

Yes. Every wallet gets one empty token account (ATA) closed with a 0% platform fee as a lifetime proof. From ATA #2 onward the fee is 2.0%, charged atomically inside the same transaction you sign, so nothing is held by us.
