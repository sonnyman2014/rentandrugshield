Frequently Asked Questions

Can a Token-2022 PermanentDelegate steal tokens out of a Ledger hardware wallet?

Yes—for that specific Token-2022 mint! While your SOL and other SPL tokens remain completely safe behind your Ledger private key, the spl-token-2022 runtime explicitly authorizes a mint's PermanentDelegate to execute TransferChecked or BurnChecked on any token account of that mint without the wallet owner's signature.

Can I use SPL Revoke (0x05) to remove a PermanentDelegate from my token account?

No. Standard SPL Revoke (0x05) only clears the local delegate field at bytes [72..108] of your individual token account. PermanentDelegate lives permanently inside the Mint account's TLV header (ExtensionType 12) and overrides individual account settings across the entire token supply.

Can a PermanentDelegate also steal the 0.00203928+ SOL rent inside my ATA?

No. PermanentDelegate grants transfer and burn authority over token units, not CloseAccount authority over your ATA's lamports (unless you manually reassigned account close_authority). Once your token balance hits zero, you can still close the ATA yourself to recover your full rent deposit.

Can a developer renounce PermanentDelegate if it was accidentally enabled?

Only the active PermanentDelegate itself can call SetAuthority (AuthorityType::PermanentDelegate = 12) to set the authority to None (zeroes). Until bytes [170..202] read all zeroes on-chain, Layer-10 treats the mint as an active seizure risk.
