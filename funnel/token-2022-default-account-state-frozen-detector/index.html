Frequently Asked Questions

What does DefaultAccountState::Frozen do on a Solana Token-2022 mint?

Extension Type 6 (DefaultAccountState) instructs the Token-2022 program to set byte 108 of every newly created Associated Token Account to 0x02 (Frozen) at the exact moment of creation. Unless a whitelisted authority thaws your account, you can never transfer, burn, or close that token account.

Can I recover SOL rent from a NonTransferable (soulbound) spam token?

Yes, as long as the account is not also Frozen (byte 108 == 0x01)! While spl-token-2022 blocks transfers on NonTransferable accounts, it explicitly permits the owner to Burn (0x08) the soulbound balance to zero and execute CloseAccount (0x09) to reclaim all 2,101,920+ lamports.

Why cannot any tool close an ATA whose state byte equals 0x02 (Frozen)?

It is a hard consensus rule inside both SPL Tokenkeg and Token-2022: Processor::process_close_account checks is_frozen() and unconditionally throws TokenError::AccountFrozen unless the mint's freeze_authority signs a ThawAccount (0x0b) instruction first. Quarantining frozen ATAs keeps all your other 2,039,280-lamport accounts closing cleanly.

Why is MintCloseAuthority (Extension Type 3) risky for traders?

MintCloseAuthority lets a 32-byte authority delete the Mint account itself once total supply reaches zero. Combined with PermanentDelegate (Extension Type 12), an attacker can burn all holder balances to zero and close the Mint account, wiping token metadata from standard explorers.
