Why Closed-Source Cleaner Contracts Trigger Ledger Error 0x6808 & Force Blind Signing

Inside Ledger's Secure Element, the official app-solana BOLOS firmware parses raw transaction payloads sent over WebHID or BLE APDUs (CLA=0xE0, INS=0x06) using a strict C instruction whitelist. It can only human-decode native primitives: SystemProgram, StakeProgram, VoteProgram, ComputeBudget, and canonical SPL Token (TokenkegQfeZyiNwAJbNbGKPFXCWuBvf9Ss623VQ5DA). Why do v0 Versioned Transactions with Address Lookup Tables fail clear-signing on Ledger?
