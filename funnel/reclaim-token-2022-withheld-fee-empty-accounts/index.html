Frequently Asked Questions

Why does my empty Token-2022 account fail to close with Custom Program Error 0x20?

Hex error 0x20 (decimal 32) in spl-token-2022 is TokenError::AccountHasWithheldTransferFees. Even when your spendable balance at bytes [64..72] is zero, taxed incoming transfers leave withheld fee units inside bytes [174..182], blocking CloseAccount until those withheld units are harvested to the Mint.

Do I need the token creator's signature to run HarvestWithheldTokensToMint?

No! Unlike WithdrawWithheldTokensFromAccounts ([0x1a, 0x03]), which requires the developer's withdraw_withheld_authority, HarvestWithheldTokensToMint ([0x1a, 0x04]) is 100% permissionless—anyone can invoke it inside the same transaction right before closing their own ATA.

What if the developer closed or froze the Mint—can HarvestWithheldTokensToMint still run?

HarvestWithheldTokensToMint writes the swept fee counter into the Mint account's TransferFeeConfig struct, which succeeds whenever the Mint exists on-chain (even if mint_authority and transfer_fee_config_authority were revoked). Only if your individual ATA itself was frozen (byte 108 == 0x02) is closing blocked.

Why do Token-2022 tax accounts refund 0.00215760 SOL instead of 0.00203928 SOL?

Solana rent scales at 6,960 lamports per byte (including 128 bytes of account metadata overhead). Adding 1 AccountType byte, 4 ImmutableOwner bytes, and 12 TransferFeeAmount bytes expands a 165-byte ATA to 182 bytes, increasing recoverable rent from 2,039,280 to 2,157,600 lamports.
